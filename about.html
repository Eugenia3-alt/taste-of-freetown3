About Taste of Freetown

Who We Are

Inspired by Freetown

Taste of Freetown is a restaurant concept created to celebrate the rich food culture of Sierra Leone.

Our goal is to create a welcoming space where guests can enjoy traditional flavours, friendly service, and memorable experiences.

From family meals to special celebrations, we believe food brings people together.

Meet the Owner

Eugenia Abibatu Aminata Maxwell

I created Taste of Freetown to celebrate the rich flavours and warm hospitality of Sierra Leone. Inspired by meals shared with family and friends, they imagined a welcoming place where guests could enjoy favourites like jollof rice, grilled fish, and cassava-leaf stew. Their hope is that everyone who visits feels at home, shares a good meal, and leaves with a little taste of Freetown.

I started the restaurant to celebrate Sierra Leonean food and the joy of bringing people together over a meal. They hope guests feel welcomed, enjoy the local flavours, and leave with a sense of connection to Freetown and its warm hospitality.
